# Glossary

The words Chat's code and docs use, and what each one means here.

| Term         | Means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Attachment   | A file sent with a question: an image the model sees, shrunk in the browser, or a **document** (a PDF or text file) the model reads as its text, taken out in the browser and cut at 100,000 characters. Picked with +, pasted or dropped, up to 4 a question                                                                          |
| Delete       | Removing a chat for good: its title from the chat list and every message from its agent. It leaves the list at once, and Undo in the toast brings it back until the toast closes                                                                                                                                                       |
| Edit         | Rewriting a question already sent, its words and its attachments (✕ takes one out, Add files adds more). The new question replaces the old, and every reply and question after it goes; then the reply is asked again on the model the picker shows. No earlier version is kept                                                        |
| Library      | The page of every attachment sent in any chat, newest first under day groups, opened from the sidebar under New Chat or from Search's actions. A row opens the chat its file came from; a deleted chat's files leave it                                                                                                                |
| Model        | One of the NVIDIA models the picker offers (`src/lib/ai/models.ts`). Its **name** is what the picker's button shows ("Ultra"), its **full name** what its list shows ("Nemotron 3 Ultra"), its **key** is what a message's metadata stores (`ultra`), and its **id** is what NVIDIA is asked for (`nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b`) |
| Pinned       | A chat the owner keeps at the top of the chat list, in its own group above Today, whatever its last activity                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Search       | Finding a chat from ⌘K or the search icon: by its title, and by the words in its messages (questions and replies), and by its attachments' file names (not a document's text). Opening a result opens the chat                                                                                                                         |
| Private chat | A chat that's never saved, started with Private on a new, empty chat (`/private`). It never joins the chat list, Search or the Library, has no title, and ends for good when its page is left or reloaded; its replies still count in Usage. Private can't be turned on or off once the first question is sent                         |
| Question     | A message the owner sent, in a bubble on the end side of the thread. _Avoid_: prompt                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Starter      | One of three questions offered under a new chat's composer. A tap puts its words in the composer, sending nothing; they go once there's anything to send. Fixed, not random                                                                                                                                                            |
| Title        | A chat's name in the chat list and its pill. A chat has none ("New chat") until Lightning names it after the first reply. Once a chat has a title, by Lightning or a rename, it is kept: Lightning never names it again. _Avoid_: name                                                                                                 |
